A multiplayer chatroom where you are your cursor! Move your mouse to show your position, chat with others in real-time using WebRTC peer-to-peer connections. Built with Canvas API for smooth rendering.

**How to use:**
1. Open the chatroom and wait for initialization
2. Click "Create Offer" to start a connection
3. Copy the offer and share it with a friend (via Discord, email, etc.)
4. Have your friend paste your offer and submit it
5. They will get an "answer" - copy and paste that back into your chatroom
6. Once connected, move your mouse to see your cursor, and watch as others' cursors appear
7. Type messages in the chat box to communicate
