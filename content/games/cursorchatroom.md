A multiplayer chatroom where you are your cursor! Move your mouse to show your position, chat with others in real-time using WebRTC peer-to-peer connections. Built with pixi.js for smooth rendering.

**How to use:**
1. Open the chatroom and wait for your Peer ID to be generated
2. Share your Peer ID with friends
3. Enter their Peer ID in the input box and click "Connect"
4. Move your mouse to see your cursor, and watch as others' cursors appear
5. Type messages in the chat box to communicate
