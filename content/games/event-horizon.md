Immerse yourself in an epic sci-fi space RPG with thrilling sandbox elements. Explore the vast galaxy, building your fleet and taking on diverse missions. With millions of stars to discover, the strategic possibilities are endless. Equip your ships with a selection of 250 distinct modules and weapons, creating countless build variations that drastically alter gameplay. Both ships and modules can be upgraded, allowing for continuous evolution of your fleet.

Engage with different factions, taking on missions or capturing their bases by force. Experience a wide range of activities, from intense explorations and enemy base assaults to formidable boss battles and survival challenges. The game offers an easy start to help you get familiar with the mechanics, but brace yourself for increasingly challenging encounters as you progress into the late game. Your strategic prowess and adaptability will be put to the test as you strive to dominate the stars.

Key Features:

    150+ Unique Ships: Each with a grid-like layout for limitless customization. 
    250+ Modules and Weapons: Equip and upgrade your ships with a vast array of options to suit your playstyle. 
    Endless Build Possibilities: Countless combinations for creating unique ship configurations. 
    Upgradeable Ships and Modules: Continuously enhance your fleet's power and capabilities.
    Diverse Missions: Engage in explorations, enemy base assaults, survival challenges, and more. 
    Faction Interactions: Meet different factions, take missions from them, or capture their bases.
    Massive Galaxy: Millions of stars to explore and conquer. 
    Modding Support: Create and share mods with the community, with dozens already available. 
    Open Source: Access the game's source code on GitHub.

Prepare for an unforgettable journey as you explore the galaxy, build your fleet, and conquer the cosmos!

For the latest builds, bug reports, feature requests, and more, visit [Event Horizon on GitHub](https://github.com/PavelZinchenko/event-horizon-main)
